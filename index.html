Differential Equations I

A guided mathematical text for modeling motion, change, and structure through analytic reasoning and computational experiment.

Begin with Level I, continue into Level II, and read each module as a chapter in a working notebook.

Course Chapters

  1. 01 — Plotting Fundamentals

    Build geometric intuition with slope fields, trajectories, and numerical sketches that translate symbolic equations into visual behavior.

    Beginner · 2–3 Hours

  2. 02 — First-Order Models

    Study separable and linear equations through physical models, then compare exact forms with computational approximations.

    Beginner · 3–4 Hours

  3. 03 — Linear Systems

    Use eigenvalues and phase portraits to classify coupled dynamics and connect algebraic structure to long-term behavior.

    Intermediate · 4 Hours

  4. 04 — Partial Differential Equations

    Move from ordinary equations to diffusion and wave phenomena with Fourier methods, boundary conditions, and simulation workflows.

    Advanced · 5+ Hours

Learning Objectives

  • Formulate differential equations from physical, biological, and engineered systems.
  • Solve canonical ODE classes analytically and verify assumptions computationally.
  • Interpret phase portraits, stability, and asymptotic behavior with mathematical precision.
  • Implement numerical methods and critique approximation error in context.
  • Read, write, and present solution arguments in a clear mathematical style.
